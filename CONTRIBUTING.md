# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are all welcome.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with your platform, 1EYE version (Settings or About) and the steps to reproduce.
- **Ideas:** open an issue describing the problem you want solved.
- **Code:** fork, make your change in `desktop/` (Go) or `android/`, run `go vet ./...` in `desktop/`, and open a pull request explaining what changed and why.
- **Security problems:** don't open a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree your contribution is released under the project's [MIT License](LICENSE), and you confirm you have the right to submit it. Please follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
