Questions[ Source on GitHub ]
Is 1EYE free?

Yes. Everything 1EYE makes is free and open source under the MIT License. No ads, no accounts, no paid tier.

What is Telepathic Data Transfer?

1EYE's first project: an app that works like AirDrop for Windows, Android, macOS and Linux. Open it on your computer, scan the QR code with your phone, and send files, text and links between them over Wi-Fi or from anywhere.

Do my files go to a cloud server?

No. Files go straight from one of your devices to another and are saved on your own computer or phone. There is no 1EYE server. Cloud storage is optional and uses your own Google Drive or WebDAV account.

Can I send files when my phone and computer are on different networks?

Yes. Press Go online and 1EYE gives your computer a private https link, protected by your PIN, that works from mobile data or any other Wi-Fi.

Does it work with iPhone?

Yes, through Safari. Scan the QR code shown by 1EYE on your computer with the iPhone camera and the 1EYE page opens. No separate iPhone app needed.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit. Files stream straight to disk, so the limit is the free space on the receiving device.

Why does Windows say Unknown publisher?

The Windows app isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. The source code is public on GitHub.

How can I contribute or propose a project?

Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Bug reports, ideas and code are all welcome.
