02 · First contact

Three steps. Ten seconds.

STEP 01

Open 1EYE

Double-click on Windows, Mac or Linux, or tap the app on Android. It shows a QR code and a PIN.

STEP 02

Scan the eye

Point any phone camera at the QR code, enter the PIN and name the device. That's the whole setup.

STEP 03

Send anything

Files, text and links show up on every linked device at once. Press Go online when you're away from home.

06 · Questions

Questions from Earth.

Is 1EYE free?

Yes. 1EYE is free and open source under the MIT License. No ads, no account and no paid tier.

Is there an AirDrop for Windows and Android?

1EYE works like AirDrop for Windows, Android, macOS and Linux. Open it on your PC, scan the QR code with your phone, and drop files, text and links between them.

Do my files go to a cloud server?

No. Files go straight from one of your devices to another and are saved on your own PC or phone. There is no 1EYE server. Cloud storage is optional and uses your own Google Drive or WebDAV account.

Can I send files when my phone and PC are on different networks?

Yes. Press Go online and 1EYE gives your PC a private https link, protected by your PIN, that works from mobile data or any other Wi-Fi.

Does it work with iPhone?

Yes, through Safari. Scan the QR code shown by 1EYE on your PC with the iPhone camera and the 1EYE page opens. No separate iPhone app needed.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit. Files stream straight to disk, so the limit is the free space on the receiving device.

Why does Windows say "Unknown publisher"?

1EYE isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. The source code is public on GitHub.

Do I need to install anything?

No installer. On Windows, macOS and Linux it's a single file you run. On Android you install the APK. Other devices just use their browser.
