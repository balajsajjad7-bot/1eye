What it does

Everything you keep sending yourself. Now in one place.

Files of any size

Drag, drop or paste. Streams straight to disk with live speed. Preview photos, video, audio and PDFs.

Private chat

A live channel between your own devices. Any link you send is saved automatically.

Links shelf

One place for every URL you want open on another screen. Notes, search, one-tap open.

Wi-Fi or anywhere

Instant on your local network. One click gives a secure https link — or your own permanent domain.

PIN-locked

Six digits, rate-limited, kick any device instantly. Host controls never leave your own machine.

Built-in AI

Ask 1EYE AI about your files, chat and links, or type @eye in chat. Powered by Groq with your own free key, which stays on your PC.

8 themes

Void, Obsidian, Aurora, Ember, Ocean, Sakura, Terminal and the light Frost theme, with the 3D eye recoloured for each.

Phone as a server

The Android app hosts too — share straight from Gallery or WhatsApp into 1EYE.

How it works

Three steps. No accounts.

Open 1EYE

Double-click on Windows or tap the app on Android. The control panel opens with a QR code and a PIN.

Scan the eye

Point any phone camera at the QR, enter the PIN, name the device. That's the whole setup.

Send anything

Files, text, links — they show up on every linked device instantly. Go online when you're away from home.

Privacy

Your files never touch our servers.

No cloud

Files go straight from one of your devices to another and are saved in a folder on the host. There is no 1EYE server in between.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no email, no analytics inside the app. A 6-digit PIN decides who may connect, and you can kick any device.

Anywhere mode is opt-in

Only when you press Go online does 1EYE open an encrypted tunnel, through Cloudflare or another relay you can see named on screen.
