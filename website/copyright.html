Copyright and takedown policy

Effective 18 November 2026

1EYE does not host anyone's files. Content shared with 1EYE stays on the sharer's own devices, and public cloud links are hosted by the cloud provider (for example Google Drive), not by us. We can't see or remove that content.

Where to report

Where the content is Report to
A public Google Drive link made with 1EYE Google's copyright removal form
A WebDAV provider (Koofr, InfiniCLOUD, Nextcloud…) That provider's abuse or copyright contact
A host reached through a trycloudflare.com, localhost.run or pinggy link The tunnel provider's abuse contact (Cloudflare: abuse.cloudflare.com)
The 1eye.dpdns.org website, the 1EYE source code, or our social media accounts Us, as below

Reporting content we control

Open an issue at https://github.com/balajsajjad7-bot/1eye/issues titled "Copyright notice" (or ask there for a private contact) with:

  1. The work you say is infringed, and who owns it
  2. The exact link to the material on our site, repository or account
  3. Your contact details
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised, and that your notice is accurate and you are the owner or authorised to act for them
  5. Your name as a signature

We will review it and remove material that infringes. If your material was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice with the same details.
