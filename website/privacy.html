Privacy policy

1EYE has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no update checks. There is no 1EYE server: the app runs on your own PC or phone (the host) and your other devices connect to it.

What stays on the host

Files, chat, links, the PIN, linked devices and settings are stored only in the 1EYE_Data and 1EYE_Shared folders next to the app. The AI key and the permanent-domain token are stored in 1EYE_Data/data.json, readable only by your user account, and are never sent to linked devices.

When 1EYE contacts other systems

1EYE only connects to the services below when you turn on the feature that needs them:

You do this1EYE connects toWhat is sent
Use 1EYE on Wi-FiOnly your own devices on your networkWhatever you share
Press Go onlineCloudflare (trycloudflare.com), or if that is blocked localhost.run or pinggy.ioTraffic between your devices passes through the tunnel service, encrypted with HTTPS
Press Go online for the first time on WindowsGitHub, to download Cloudflare's open-source cloudflared programA normal download request

Add a Groq key and use 1EYE AI or @eyeGroq (api.groq.com)Your question and only the context you choose (chat, links, file names, attached text files)
Any of the above when your network has no DNSCloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1)The name of the service being looked up

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Turning the feature off (Go offline, remove the domain, remove the AI key) stops 1EYE contacting it.

The website

1eye.dpdns.org is a static page hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics.
