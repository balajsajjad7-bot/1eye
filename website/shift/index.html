    Questions[ FAQ ]
    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Shift converts everything inside your browser, on your own device. Files never leave your computer or phone, so it works for private documents too.

    Which formats does Shift convert?

    Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, SVG, BMP, AVIF, ICO) to JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF or ICO. PDF to images or text, and merging PDFs. Video (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI) to MP4, WebM, GIF or audio. Audio (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC) to each other. Data (CSV, JSON, XLSX, YAML) to each other. Documents (DOCX, Markdown, text, HTML) to PDF, HTML, text or Markdown.

    Is there a file size limit?

    Only your device's memory. Images, PDFs and documents convert in seconds. Video and audio use ffmpeg compiled for the browser: the first video conversion downloads the engine (about 31 MB) once, and files up to about 1.5 GB work on most computers.

    Is Shift free?

    Yes. Free, no account, no watermark, no limits, open source. It is a 1EYE project, the open source lab behind Telepathic Data Transfer.

    How do I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone to JPG?

    Drop the HEIC files on Shift, choose JPG and press Convert. You can convert many at once and download them all as a ZIP.
