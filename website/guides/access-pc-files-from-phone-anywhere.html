Get files from your home PC on your phone, from anywhere

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

Left a file on your home PC? If 1EYE is running there, you can reach it from your phone over mobile data. No router settings, no port forwarding, no uploading everything to a cloud first.

Set it up once

  1. Run 1EYE on your home PC and link your phone by scanning the QR code.
  2. In 1EYE's Control page, press Go online. Your PC gets a private https link; scan the new QR code with your phone or save the link.
  3. For a link that never changes, add a free permanent domain in Control → My domain (optional).

Use it from anywhere

Open the link on your phone, enter your PIN, and download any file in the shared folder or send new ones home. Chat and links sync too.

Is it safe?

  • The link only works while 1EYE is online, and everyone still needs your PIN.
  • After 20 wrong PINs from the internet, sign-ins pause for 10 minutes.
  • Host controls (like changing the PIN or shutting down) only work on the PC itself.
  • You can press Go offline at any time.

Want files available when the PC is off?

Connect free cloud storage in 1EYE's Cloud page and turn on auto-backup. With Google Drive you can also make public links that keep working when your PC is shut down.

Quick answers

Can I get files from my home PC on my phone when I'm away?

Yes. Run 1EYE on the PC, press Go online, and open the private link on your phone with your PIN.

Do I need port forwarding?

No. 1EYE creates a secure tunnel link automatically.
