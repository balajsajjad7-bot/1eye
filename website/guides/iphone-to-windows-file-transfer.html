Send photos from iPhone to a Windows PC without iTunes

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

AirDrop won't reach a Windows PC, and iTunes or iCloud are slow for a few photos. With 1EYE running on your PC, your iPhone can send photos and videos over Wi-Fi straight from Safari. No app to install on the iPhone.

How to do it

  1. Download and open 1EYE for Windows. It shows a QR code and a PIN.
  2. Open the iPhone Camera and point it at the QR code. Tap the link that appears; 1EYE opens in Safari.
  3. Enter the PIN and give the iPhone a name.
  4. Tap the upload area, choose Photo Library, select photos or videos, and they upload to your PC.

About HEIC photos: Safari may convert iPhone photos to JPEG while uploading. That's usually what you want on Windows, because JPEG opens everywhere. Videos upload as they are.

Sending from PC to iPhone

Drop files on 1EYE on your PC and open the 1EYE page in Safari on your iPhone. Tap a file to download it to the Files app, or long-press a photo to save it to Photos.

Works from anywhere too

Press Go online on your PC and your iPhone can reach it over mobile data, still protected by your PIN.

Quick answers

Can I send photos from iPhone to Windows without iTunes?

Yes. Open 1EYE on the PC, scan its QR code with the iPhone camera, and upload photos from Safari over Wi-Fi.

Do I need an app on the iPhone?

No. 1EYE works in Safari on iPhone and iPad.
