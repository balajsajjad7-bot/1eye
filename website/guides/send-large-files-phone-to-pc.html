Send large videos from your phone to your PC in full quality

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

Messaging apps squeeze videos and cap file sizes. Email stops at about 25 MB. To move a 4K video or a multi-gigabyte file from your phone to your computer intact, send it directly over Wi-Fi.

The fast way: direct over Wi-Fi

  1. Open 1EYE on your computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
  2. Connect your phone by scanning the QR code and entering the PIN.
  3. Choose the video on your phone. It streams straight to the PC's disk with live speed shown, in the original quality.

There is no fixed size limit: files are written to disk as they arrive, so the only limit is free space on the PC.

How long will it take?

FileTypical home Wi-Fi (about 20 MB/s)
1-minute 4K video (about 400 MB)About 20 seconds
10-minute 4K video (about 4 GB)About 3 to 4 minutes
Photo library export (20 GB)About 17 minutes

Speeds depend on your router and how close you are; 5 GHz Wi-Fi is much faster than 2.4 GHz.

When you're not at home

Turn on Go online in 1EYE and send from anywhere through a private https link. It's slower than local Wi-Fi because it runs through your internet connection. For files you want to keep online, connect free cloud storage (Google Drive, Koofr or InfiniCLOUD) in 1EYE's Cloud page.

Quick answers

How do I send a large video from phone to PC without losing quality?

Send it directly over Wi-Fi with an app like 1EYE. It transfers the original file without compression or a size limit.

Is there a file size limit in 1EYE?

No fixed limit; it is limited only by free disk space on the receiving device.
