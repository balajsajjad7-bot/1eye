Share files between phone and PC without internet

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

You don't need internet access to move files between devices that are close together. A local Wi-Fi network, even a router with no internet or your phone's hotspot, is enough.

Option 1: any Wi-Fi router, even offline

  1. Connect your PC and phone to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open 1EYE on the PC and scan its QR code with the phone.
  3. Send files, text and links. Everything stays on your local network.

Option 2: use your phone's hotspot

  1. Turn on the hotspot on your phone (mobile data can be off).
  2. Connect the PC to the phone's hotspot.
  3. Open 1EYE on the PC and scan the QR code. Transfers go directly between the two devices.

Or let the phone host: the 1EYE Android app can run the server itself. Start it on the phone and open the address it shows in the PC's browser.

Good for

  • Travel, trains and flights with no signal
  • Offices or schools where cloud services are blocked
  • Moving big files without using mobile data

Quick answers

Can I transfer files without internet?

Yes. 1EYE works over any local Wi-Fi network or a phone hotspot with no internet connection.

Does it use mobile data?

Not on a local network. Only Anywhere mode uses the internet.
