Transfer files from Android to PC without a USB cable

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

You don't need a cable, Bluetooth or a cloud upload to move files from Android to your computer. Over your home Wi-Fi it takes seconds, in full quality, with a free app called 1EYE.

Step by step

  1. On your PC, download and open 1EYE (Windows, Mac or Linux). It shows a QR code and a PIN.
  2. On your Android phone, install 1EYE.apk (Android 8 or newer). Allow "install unknown apps" for your browser when asked. You can also skip the app and just use the phone's browser.
  3. Scan the QR code, enter the PIN, and name your phone.
  4. Pick files in 1EYE, or use Share → 1EYE from Gallery, Files or any app. They arrive in the 1EYE_Shared folder on your PC.

Not on the same Wi-Fi?

Press Go online in 1EYE on your PC. It creates a private https link protected by your PIN, so your phone can send files from mobile data or any other network.

Troubleshooting

  • Phone can't open the page: make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network, and allow 1EYE through the Windows firewall when asked.
  • Slow transfers: use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band and keep the phone near the router.
