AirDrop for Windows: the free way to share files with your PC

By Balaj · Updated 20 November 2026 · 3 min read

Apple's AirDrop only works between Apple devices, so a Windows PC can't receive from it. You can get the same "drop it and it arrives" experience on Windows with a free app called 1EYE, and it works with Android phones and iPhones too.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC
  • A phone (Android or iPhone) on the same Wi-Fi, or any internet connection if you use Anywhere mode
  • About one minute

Set it up in three steps

  1. Download 1EYE for Windows. It's a single 16 MB file, 1EYE.exe, from the download page. There is nothing to install.
  2. Open it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway; 1EYE is open source but not code-signed yet. Your browser opens the 1EYE control panel with a QR code and a 6-digit PIN.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone. Enter the PIN and name the phone. Done: anything you drop on either screen appears on the other.

What you can send

  • Files of any size in full quality, photos, videos, PDFs, ZIPs
  • Text and codes through a private chat between your own devices
  • Links on a shelf you can open on the other screen in one tap

Tip: on Android, install 1EYE.apk to share straight from Gallery or any app's Share menu. On iPhone, no app is needed: the QR code opens 1EYE in Safari.

How is it different from Nearby Share or Phone Link?

1EYE doesn't need a Microsoft or Google account, Bluetooth, or a phone from a particular brand. It runs over your own Wi-Fi, keeps files on your own PC, and the same app works on Windows, Mac and Linux. When you're away from home, one click on Go online gives your PC a private https link protected by your PIN.

Is it safe?

Files go straight between your devices. Nobody can join without the PIN, you can disconnect any device at any time, and the code is public on GitHub. See the privacy policy for exactly what 1EYE connects to.

Quick answers

Is there an official AirDrop for Windows?

No. AirDrop only works between Apple devices. Apps like 1EYE give Windows the same kind of quick drop over Wi-Fi.

Does 1EYE work with iPhone?

Yes. Scan the QR code with the iPhone camera and 1EYE opens in Safari, no app needed.

Is 1EYE free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT License, with no ads or account.
