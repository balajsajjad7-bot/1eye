Brand guidelines

Effective 18 November 2026

The code is MIT-licensed and free to fork. The 1EYE name, the "Telepathic Data Transfer" tagline and the eye-in-pyramid logo identify the official 1EYE project, so they have a few rules.

You can, without asking

  • Say your app or video is about, works with, or is based on 1EYE ("built on 1EYE", "a 1EYE tutorial").
  • Use the name and unchanged logo in reviews, news, tutorials and social posts that link to 1eye.dpdns.org.
  • Share the official downloads unchanged.

Please don't

  • Publish a modified build or fork under the name 1EYE or with the 1EYE logo. Give your fork its own name and logo.
  • Use the name or logo in a way that suggests we made, endorse or sponsor your product.
  • Use a confusingly similar name, logo, domain or social handle.
  • Sell the official 1EYE downloads or bundle them with other software.

Brand assets

  • Colours: violet #8B5CF6, lavender #C4B5FD, cyan #67E8F9, night #050507
  • Logo: logo.svg. Keep its proportions and colours, on a dark background, with space around it.

Questions or a request outside these rules: open an issue at https://github.com/balajsajjad7-bot/1eye/issues.
