# Code of conduct

1EYE's issues, discussions and social channels are for building and using 1EYE together.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, sexual content, threats or doxxing.
- No spam, self-promotion unrelated to 1EYE, or piracy requests.
- Keep reports of security problems private (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

Maintainers may remove content or block anyone who breaks these rules. Report problems by opening an issue or messaging the maintainer.
